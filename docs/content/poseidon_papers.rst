POSEIDON Paper Timeline
===============

Each list includes the ADS link, a key figure from the paper displaying POSEIDON, and a list of how POSEIDON was utilized. 

.. note:: Don't see your paper below? Reach out to us! 

Pre Public Release

MacDonald & Madhusudhan (2017a)
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
`HD 209458b in new light: evidence of nitrogen chemistry, patchy clouds and sub-solar water` 
(`ADS Link <https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2017MNRAS.469.1979M/abstract>`_)

First demonstration of POSEIDON's capabilities, including free chemistry retrievals 
with patchy clouds on HD 209458b's Hubble transmission spectrum.

.. image:: ../_static/paper_images/MacDonald_Madhu_2017a.png
   :alt: Sample image
   :width: 1000
   :align: center

MacDonald & Madhusudhan (2017b)
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
`Signatures of Nitrogen Chemistry in Hot Jupiter Atmospheres`
(`ADS Link <https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2017ApJ...850L..15M/abstract>`_)

Application of POSEIDON to a population of hot Jupiters, finding evidence of
nitrogen-bearing molecules (NH3 and/or HCN) in the atmospheres of three hot Jupiters.

.. image:: ../_static/paper_images/MacDonald_Madhu_2017b.png
   :alt: Sample image
   :width: 1000
   :align: center

Sedaghati (2017)
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
`Detection of titanium oxide in the atmosphere of a hot Jupiter`
(`ADS Link <https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2017Natur.549..238S/abstract>`_)

POSEIDON identified TiO for the first time in an exoplanet atmosphere, using
VLT FORS2 transmission spectra of WASP-19b. 

.. image:: ../_static/paper_images/Sedaghati_2017.png
   :alt: Sample image
   :width: 1000
   :align: center

(`ADS Link <https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2020ApJ...893L..43M/abstract>`_)
